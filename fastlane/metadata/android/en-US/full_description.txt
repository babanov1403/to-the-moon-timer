To The Moon Timer turns Pomodoro focus sessions into a small retro arcade space journey.

Start a focus session to launch your spaceship, fly through a calm cosmic scene, and land on a new pixel-art planet when the session is complete. Take a break, then continue through the Pomodoro cycle until a full set is done.

Features:
- Retro space-themed Pomodoro timer.
- Adjustable focus sessions from 10 to 90 minutes.
- Default 25-minute focus sessions and 5-minute breaks.
- Four focus sessions per Pomodoro set.
- Pause, resume, restart, and duration picker flows.
- Local focus-time statistics.
- No ads, no tracking, and no network services.
